# AI Roadmap

Rysmaan's AI roadmap is focused on practical procurement moments: drafting RFQs, finding qualified vendors, comparing quotes, spotting risk, and keeping follow-ups moving. The goal is simple: reduce manual work while keeping project managers and service providers in control.

## Roadmap at a Glance

| Moment | Feature | Users | What Users Get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare quotes | AI Quote Comparison Summary | Project managers | A clean summary of vendor pricing, schedules, gaps, exclusions, and next steps. |
| Draft an RFQ | AI RFQ Builder | Project managers | A ready-to-edit RFQ draft from a short project or package description. |
| Find vendors | AI Vendor Shortlist Recommendations | Project managers | Suggested vendors with clear fit reasons based on services, location, status, and qualifications. |
| Avoid risk | AI Risk Flags | Project managers | Timely warnings for missing documents, vague scopes, unusually low bids, and compliance gaps. |
| Follow up | AI Follow-Up Questions | Project managers and vendors | Specific clarification messages that keep quote review moving inside Rysmaan. |

## 1. AI Quote Comparison Summary

When quotes come in, Rysmaan will help project managers see the decision clearly.

What users can expect:
- Price, schedule, and scope differences summarized in one view.
- Missing documents and unclear vendor notes called out.
- Exclusions and assumptions surfaced before award.
- A clean summary that can be shared with stakeholders.

## 2. AI RFQ Builder

Project managers will be able to turn a short description into a structured RFQ draft.

What users can expect:
- Suggested scope language.
- Recommended service categories.
- Required documents and quote requirements.
- Suggested pricing breakdowns and vendor instructions.
- Full editing control before anything is sent.

## 3. AI Vendor Shortlist Recommendations

Rysmaan will help project managers start with the right vendor list for each RFQ.

What users can expect:
- Vendor recommendations based on service fit, location, preferred status, ratings, and qualification completeness.
- Plain-language reasons for each suggested vendor.
- Early visibility into qualification or insurance gaps.

## 4. AI Risk Flags

Rysmaan will help teams catch procurement risk before it becomes expensive.

What users can expect:
- Warnings for unusually low bids, missing non-negotiable responses, expired insurance, and vague scope language.
- Clear next steps for what to review or ask.
- Risk notes shown where users are already working.

## 5. AI Follow-Up Questions

When a quote is incomplete, Rysmaan will help project managers ask better questions faster.

What users can expect:
- Drafted clarification messages based on quote gaps.
- Specific asks for missing documents, unclear pricing, scope exclusions, or schedule commitments.
- Editable messages that keep vendor communication inside the platform.

## Product Principles

- AI should draft, summarize, and highlight. Users make the final decision.
- Suggestions should be clear, structured, and easy to edit.
- Every recommendation should point back to the RFQ, quote, message, or document it came from.
- The experience should feel like a helpful part of the existing workflow, not a separate tool to learn.
